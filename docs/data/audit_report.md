## 7. Limitations

- The export has no source URLs or document IDs, so split groups are derived from content and topic (weaker than document-level grouping). Leakage is reduced, not proven absent.
- Near-duplicate candidates need >= 5 shared rare shingles (df <= 10); answers shorter than 5 words, or overlapping only on common shingles, are matched only exactly.
- Boilerplate answers are allowed in several splits by design; they are flagged `boilerplate_answer`.
- A duplicate group can hold near-identical answers about different conditions (counted above); collapsing retrieval hits by `duplicate_group_id` alone can therefore hide the condition asked about.
